## 🧠 Why This Matters

### ROI Confusion:

**What "180x ROI" actually means:**
- ROI = Return on Investment
- 180x = 180 times your investment
- $3,150 × 180 = $567,000

**Why it's confusing:**
1. Most parents don't calculate this automatically
2. "180x" sounds exaggerated/unbelievable
3. Technical term creates mental friction
4. People skip over things they don't understand

**Why "$100K+" is better:**
1. Immediate understanding — no math needed
2. Conservative estimate — more believable
3. Clear dollar amount — tangible benefit
4. "Lifetime" clarifies it's long-term value

---

### Top Businessman Emphasis:

**Before:** "live instruction + project work"
- Sounds like any teacher
- Generic educational experience
- Doesn't differentiate from competitors

**After:** "project work + live instruction from top businessman"
- Emphasizes instructor quality
- Shows real-world experience
- Justifies premium pricing
- Differentiates from typical coding schools

## 📝 Copywriting Lessons

### Rule #1: No Jargon
If your grandmother wouldn't understand it, rewrite it.
- ❌ "180x ROI"
- ✅ "$100K+ lifetime career value"

### Rule #2: Be Specific
Vague = Forgettable. Specific = Memorable.
- ❌ "live instruction"
- ✅ "live instruction from top businessman"

### Rule #3: Show, Don't Calculate
Give them the answer, not the math homework.
- ❌ "180x" (requires mental calculation)
- ✅ "$100K+" (immediate understanding)
