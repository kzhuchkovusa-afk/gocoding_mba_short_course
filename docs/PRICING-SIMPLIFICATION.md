# Pricing Section Simplification
## Date: December 24, 2024

### Overview
Removed two prominent sections from pricing to simplify the page and focus on the core value proposition.

---

## Changes Made

### 1. ✅ Removed "Early Bird Pricing Alert"

**What was removed:**
- Large orange/yellow alert box at top of pricing section
- Lightning bolt icon with pulsating animation
- Progressive pricing explanation ($350 → $450 → $550 → $650)
- "Lock in the lowest price forever" messaging

**Why removed:**
- User requested simplification
- Too much emphasis on urgency/scarcity
- Cleaner, more straightforward pricing presentation

---

### 2. ✅ Removed "Breaking Down the Value"

**What was removed:**
- Section title: "💰 Breaking Down the Value"
- Subtitle: "What does your investment really mean?"
- Three metric cards:
  1. **$17.50** — Cost per hour
  2. **$3,150** — Total investment
  3. **180x** — Return on investment

**Why removed:**
- User requested simplification
- Too many numbers/metrics
- Focused messaging on simple pricing structure

---

### 3. ✅ Kept "What Even an Average Student Gets"

**What remains:**
- Section title: "🚀 What Even an 'Average' Student Gets:"
- Six benefit items with check icons:
  - Working MVP
  - Portfolio piece for college
  - Coding & design skills
  - Public speaking confidence
  - Business fundamentals
  - Network of peers
- Bottom note: "For just $17.50/hour, your child gets all this..."

**Why kept:**
- User specifically requested to keep this
- Shows concrete, achievable outcomes
- Emphasizes value without overwhelming with numbers
- Simple, clear messaging
