This is a single-page website (landing page) designed to attract parents to enroll their children in the MBA for Kids year-long program. The program teaches children to create real IT projects, develops entrepreneurial thinking, and public speaking skills.

## 🎯 Target Audience

**Primary audience**: Parents of children aged 11-14 who:
- Are concerned about their child's future
- Want to develop confidence and leadership skills
- Are looking for an alternative to endless social media time
- Are ready to invest in their child's education
- Value a practical approach to learning

**Audience pain points:**
- Bullying and loss of confidence at school
- Excessive use of social media and gadgets
- Lack of skills for the future job market
- Fear that the child won't be ready for adult life
