# ✅ COMPLETE: How It Works Section Redesign

## Summary

Successfully redesigned the "How It Works" section with a new 4-phase structure and added a detailed weekly schedule table. All content written in simple, achievable language emphasizing modern tools that kids actually enjoy using.

---

## What Was Done

### 1. ✅ Expanded to 4 Detailed Phases

**Old:** 3 broad phases (Months 1-3, 4-6, 7-9)

**New:** 4 specific phases with clear focus areas:

1. **Months 1-2: The Foundation** (Business & Ideation)
   - Learn revenue models, market research, idea validation
   - Outcome: Validated business idea + one-page business plan
   - Tools: Miro, Google Slides, Business Model Canvas

2. **Months 3-6: The Build** (Product Development)
   - UI/UX design, prototyping, coding basics (Python/JavaScript)
   - Outcome: Working MVP (game, website, or app)
   - Tools: Figma, Replit, Trello

3. **Months 7-8: The Launch** (Marketing & Sales)
   - Go-to-market strategy, digital marketing, sales funnels
   - Outcome: Investor pitch deck + marketing plan
   - Tools: Canva, Google Analytics, Mailchimp

4. **Month 9: The Pitch** (Competition & Graduation)
   - Public speaking, storytelling, handling Q&A
   - Outcome: Polished pitch to real entrepreneurs for $3,000
   - Final: Graduation, certificates, portfolio piece
