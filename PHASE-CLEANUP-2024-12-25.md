## 📊 Benefits of Simplified Format

1. **Faster to Read** - No need to scan through bullet points
2. **Mobile-Friendly** - Less vertical space, better mobile UX
3. **Professional Look** - Clean, concise, business-like
4. **Easy to Translate** - Shorter text = easier localization
5. **Clear Structure** - Three distinct sections (Learn → Outcome → Tools)

---

## ❌ Removed Elements

### Deleted from All Phases:
- ❌ Bullet points with icons (`<li><i class="fas fa-check"></i>`)
- ❌ Long explanatory descriptions
- ❌ Parenthetical notes in tools section
- ❌ Extra emphasis text (like "The Key Skill: Articulating Yourself")

### Why Removed:
- Too verbose for dropdown content
- Creates visual clutter
- Slows down information scanning
- Mobile readability issues
