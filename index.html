How It Works

Your Child's 9-Month Journey from Idea to Investor Pitch

A proven step-by-step system that transforms curious kids into confident young entrepreneurs

01

Months 1-2: Game On! Business Ideas

Business & Ideation (Game-Based Learning)

What they learn: Business Models, Market Research, Profit & Loss, Idea Validation.

Key Outcome: A validated business idea and a one-page business plan.
Tools: Miro, Google Slides, Business Model Canvas.
02

Months 3-6: Build Time!

Product Development (Step-by-Step, Everyone Succeeds!)

What they learn: UI/UX Design, Prototyping, Coding (Python/JS basics), Project Management.

Key Outcome: A working Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of their app, game, or website.
Tools: Figma, Replit, Trello.
03

Months 7-8: Time to Be Heard!

Marketing & Sales (Learn to Be Heard!)

What they learn: Go-to-Market Strategy, Digital Marketing, Sales Funnels, Pitch Deck Creation.

Key Outcome: A complete investor pitch deck and a simple marketing plan.
Tools: Canva, Google Analytics, Mailchimp.
04

Your Child's Moment to Shine

The Grand Finale: Competition & Graduation

What they learn: Public Speaking, Storytelling Under Pressure, Q&A Mastery.

Key Outcome: A confident, polished 5-minute pitch delivered to a panel of real entrepreneurs and investors — competing for $1,000 in seed funding. This is their spotlight moment: standing on stage, owning their story, and proving they can build something real.
The Celebration: Official graduation ceremony, achievement certificates, and a portfolio-ready project they'll proudly showcase for college applications, internships, and future opportunities.
